Why ashrinka is not in the rip formula

NRF 1.6% is retail sales-value loss, FY2022, last official number. A stolen Walmart box is usually opened. A crushed box is usually discounted and opened. That is untracked opening, not disappearance.

Physical destruction (fire, water, landfill) is the only terminal loss of units. Default 0.25% of print E. Accounting shrink is tracked separately and does not shrink the GMR denominator.

Sensitivity | what moves the conclusion

Largest effects on remaining-chasability (GMRs per 10k accessible sealed): R posterior (sample n,k), then collation (small at these R), then closet share (changes accessible count, not density if random), then observed hits a expected (the only thing that changes remaining density without changing R). Opening more boxes at the expected rate does not make leftover boxes colder.
